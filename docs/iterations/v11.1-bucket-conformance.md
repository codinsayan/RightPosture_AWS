# Version 11.1 — Checkpoint crediting by majority

Status: in-progress — implemented and unit tested; device gate not yet run.  
Source: §6.12.4; follows 4.1 and the pose-conformance work

Scope: credit a trajectory checkpoint on a **majority** of its frames being conformant, instead of
on a single one.

Independent of the 10.x ghost series. This can ship on its own.

Today a bucket stays credited once *any* frame in it was conformant. That answers "was the patient
ever in the right position in this quarter of the range", and a patient who is out of position for
three quarters of a bucket and passes through the right pose once on the way answers yes. That is
the compound-exercise hole: the dominant joint travels legally, the omitted joint happens to sweep
through the right angle somewhere in each bucket, and the rep counts.

Tests: right one frame in four throughout is refused as `SKIPPED`; one frame in seventeen — landmark
jitter — still counts; a faithful rep is unchanged and reports full conformance.

Device gate: a deliberately half-performed compound exercise is refused, and a correctly performed
one counts. Run both; the second matters as much as the first.

## Result

- `core/live/PhaseTracker.kt` — the two per-leg checkpoint bitmasks become four `IntArray`s
  (frames seen and frames conformant, per bucket, per leg). New `markCheckpoint(conformant)` and
  `creditedMask(frames, agreed)`; `clearCheckpoints()` resets them from both `startRep` and
  `clearRep`. `countFrame` now returns early past `phi = 1`, so an overshoot neither credits nor
  discredits a bucket.
- `core/Params.kt` — `TRAJECTORY_MIN_BUCKET_CONFORMANT_FRACTION` 0.6.
- `TrajectoryConformanceTest` — 7 cases.

No change to `CompletedRep`'s shape, so nothing in front of the seam needed touching: the whole
iteration is behind `FrameResult`.

Commands:

- `gradlew testDebugUnitTest` — BUILD SUCCESSFUL; 774 tests, 0 failures (767 before).

Device gate: **not yet run.** It needs a two-joint exercise performed twice on a phone, once
correctly and once with the second joint deliberately left out through most of the movement.

## Traps

- **Count every valid frame in the denominator, not only the agreeing ones.** A denominator built
  from the conformant frames makes every bucket unanimous by construction.
- **Keep `TRAJECTORY_CHECKPOINTS` at 4.** Its comment in `Params.kt` sizes four against the device
  frame rate and the fastest rep the duration bound accepts. Finer buckets mean fewer frames each
  and a noisier fraction, which trades one failure mode for another.
- **Overshoot must abstain, and this is the one that will bite.** Past `phi = 1` the phase index
  saturates at 50, so a patient swinging beyond what the physio recorded is scored against the
  turnaround while being well past it, and every joint that moves with the dominant one reads as a
  deviation it never made. That is an artefact of the overshoot, not a second fault, and
  `RepNote.Overshot` already has something more useful to say about it. Track judged frames
  separately from valid frames and stop judging past `phi = 1` — not past
  `1 + REP_OVERSHOOT_TOLERANCE`, which answers a different question.

  Without this, `RepNoteTest`'s two overshoot cases fail: the rep is rejected as `SKIPPED` instead of
  being counted with a note. That is a real regression, not a test to update.
- The existing comment in `PoseConformanceTest` about a bucket staying credited on any single frame
  becomes stale — update it rather than leaving two rules documented.
