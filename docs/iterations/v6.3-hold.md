## What is deliberately not reused

§6.19 is explicit that the cyclic definitions "would select on noise and produce a meaningless watch
list", because both the §6.7.1 dominant feature and the §6.10 salience are defined through range of
motion, and a hold's range of motion is near zero by construction. So:

| Cyclic | Hold |
|---|---|
| dominant = largest ROM | `argmax |μ_j − θ_j(neutral)|` — furthest from neutral |
| salience = ROM/σ | `1 / max(σ, floor)` — tightness |
| watch-list filters 1–4 | filters 2 and 3 only; 1 and 4 are ROM-based |
| phase index, `σ_eff` inflation | none — there is no phase to be uncertain about |

`PhaseTracker` is not used at all. What *is* shared: the §6.9.4 sigma floor and cap, the §6.17.1
frame-score shape, and §6.18's CUSUM, which §6.19 runs over `FrameScore` sampled at 1 Hz instead of
per rep.

## Bugs found and fixed this iteration

- **A phone bump mid-rep was banking a false "too slow".** §6.20.2's pause withholds frames from the
  engine, but the rep in progress kept its start timestamp, so on resume it completed carrying the
  whole pause in its duration. Measured: an 8-second pause produced a 10.9 s rep refused as
  `TOO_SLOW`. `PhaseTracker` now abandons the rep in progress when the gap between frames exceeds
  `OCCLUSION_IDLE_SECONDS` — the same rule §6.12.3 already applies when the person leaves — and the
  camera screen calls `onPersonLost()` on the edge into a pause so the rep is dropped immediately
  rather than on resume. Reps already banked are kept either way.
- **The hold timer kept running past the break** (found by its own test). `lastNs` advanced on every
  frame including after the break, so `holdDuration` counted the time the patient spent sitting on
  the floor. The end time is now frozen at the break.
- **A transient read as drift** (found by its own test). §6.19 says the slope must be *sustained*
  and the first implementation ignored the word. A least-squares fit over the whole hold spikes the
  moment a transient lands and only flattens once enough later samples arrive, so a 0.4 s wobble
  ended a hold the patient had already recovered from. Drift now has to hold above threshold for
  `HOLD_DRIFT_SUSTAINED_SECONDS`.
- `ExercisePanel`'s fatigue state was not cleared when a set was finished.

## Notes

- **The stability precondition is checked after the dominant joint is chosen, not before.** §6.19's
  8° MAD bound is on `θ_dom`, and which joint that is depends on the offsets, so the order matters.
- **A feature valid for a handful of frames is not a measurement of the hold.** Features present in
  under half the stable window are dropped from the reference rather than averaged over whatever
  frames happened to have them.
- **An abstaining joint neither scores nor breaks a hold.** §6.6, again: a hold must not end because
  the camera briefly lost a knee.
- **The frame score is sampled at 1 Hz, not per frame.** §6.19 wants "your form started slipping at
  0:38", so the series has to be in seconds.

## Outstanding

- **No UI entry point.** The engine, the authoring flow and the manifest all assume cyclic mode;
  `RposeManifest.mode` carries `"cyclic"` and nothing writes `"hold"`. §3.8 check 2 already "offers
  hold mode" when ROM < 15°, and that offer is where this should be reachable from. Until then hold
  mode is complete and unreachable, which is why the device gate is unrun.
- The neutral-pose capture (`θ_j(neutral_standing)`, a required one-second step of the hold
  authoring flow per §6.19) has no recording UI either; `buildHoldReference` takes it as a parameter.
- §6.19's change-point machinery over the 1 Hz series is exposed as `HoldUpdate.qualitySamples` but
  not yet run through `assessFatigue` — that needs the CUSUM entry point generalised past
  `SessionRecord`, which is 8.3's problem.
