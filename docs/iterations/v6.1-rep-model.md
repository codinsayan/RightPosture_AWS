## Notes

- **`quality` is nullable and stays nullable.** The engine previously stored `quality ?: 0.0`. That
  zero was unreachable for *valid* reps, so it never corrupted the set median — but it would have
  gone straight into §6.18.2's baseline the moment CUSUM read the records, and a median dragged down
  by an unscored rep invents a collapse that never happened. §1.6's rule is that absent means "no
  score", never zero, and this is the first place the distinction has a consumer.
- **Partial reps are excluded from `validQualitySeries` rather than zeroed.** §6.12.4 already
  decided they are not reps. A zero in the middle of the series would read to CUSUM as a
  catastrophic decline at exactly the moment the app declined to measure anything.
- **Magnitude and direction are both kept.** §6.17.1 scores on `z²`, so quality is made of
  magnitude; §6.18.5 attributes on *signed* z, because "the knee drifted 2σ into valgus" and "2σ out
  of it" are opposite findings sharing a magnitude. Reducing to one would silently pick a side.
- **`frames` is stored per sample** so `meanSignedZ` can weight properly across reps. A mean over
  three frames is not evidence equal to a mean over ninety.
- **The velocity profile is captured rather than reduced.** SPARC is a frequency-domain measure with
  no scalar to carry forward in its place. §6.18.5 permits dropping smoothness entirely; keeping the
  profile means that stays 6.2's decision rather than being made by default here, at roughly ninety
  doubles per rep.
- **`hasEnoughRepsForFatigue` counts valid reps, not finished ones.** §6.18.1's floor is about how
  much evidence exists, not how many times the patient moved.

## Outstanding

- No database. §13.1 wants a `Rep` row persisted as each rep completes so a killed app leaves a
  recoverable session; this is the in-memory half only, and 7.1 is where Room lands.
- Nothing reads `engine.session` yet — 6.2 is its first consumer. The set summary UI is 8.3.
- `FaultEvent` and `CueEvent` from §13.1 are not modelled here. They are cue-effectiveness
  concerns, not degradation prerequisites, and `CueArbiter` already emits `CueEvent` for 8.x.
