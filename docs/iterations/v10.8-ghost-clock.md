# Version 10.8 — Ghost clock

Status: in-progress — implemented and unit tested; device gate needs 10.9's overlay to run.  
Source: new work; this is what makes the ghost *lead*

Scope: a pure, JVM-testable clock that advances the ghost through the movement in real time, on
10.3's time axis. States: counting the patient in, moving, resting between reps, and waiting for a
patient who is not following.

The rest of the live pipeline is deliberately tempo-free — phase comes from the dominant joint's
value, never from a clock. That is what makes the *scoring* fair, and it is exactly why the scoring
cannot also drive a demonstration: a ghost that only moves when the patient does can never show them
a movement they have not already made. This is the one clock in the live path.

Tests: the count-in expires; a completed rep resynchronises the cycle; a patient who never moves
parks the ghost at the start pose after a bounded number of cycles and resumes when they move; a
timestamp gap does not advance it.

Device gate: a patient follows the ghost through a full prescribed set without the two drifting
apart by the end.

## Result

- `core/live/GhostClock.kt` - `GhostState` (`COUNT_IN`, `MOVING`, `RESTING`, `WAITING`, `DONE`),
  `GhostTick(state, phase, countInSeconds)`, and `GhostClock(timing).tick(timestampNs,
  patientMoving, repCompleted, setComplete)`. Pure and frame-driven: time is the camera's
  timestamps, with any gap over `GHOST_MAX_FRAME_GAP_SECONDS` (or backwards) not counted.
  - `MOVING` reads `PhaseTiming.phaseAt(elapsed)`, so the physio's pauses are reproduced.
  - After a demonstrated rep, `RESTING` for `GHOST_REST_SECONDS` at phase 0, then the next.
  - Engagement guard: a whole demonstrated cycle in which the patient never left IDLE counts as
    idle; `GHOST_IDLE_CYCLES` (1) of them parks the ghost in `WAITING` at the start pose. The
    patient moving resumes it from a fresh cycle.
  - Resync: **any** completed rep, valid or rejected, restarts the cycle at phase 0 - a rejected
    rep is still a finished movement cycle.
  - `setComplete` moves to `DONE` (phase null, nothing drawn) and it never leaves.
- `phaseLag(ghost, patient)` - ghost minus patient, taken round the cycle into [-50, 50), because
  phase 100 and phase 0 are the same pose. **Display only**; nothing in scoring reads it.
- `Params`: `GHOST_COUNT_IN_SECONDS` 3.0, `GHOST_REST_SECONDS` 0.5, `GHOST_IDLE_CYCLES` 1,
  `GHOST_MAX_FRAME_GAP_SECONDS` 0.5. All uncalibrated.
- `GhostClockTest` - 12 cases: count-in counts down and expires; a held end-range pause is held, not
  swept through; rest beat then next rep; a completed rep resynchronises; a patient 10% slow over a
  set of ten stays within 15 phase of the ghost on the last rep; a still patient parks the ghost;
  movement resumes it from the start pose; a moving patient is never waited for; a 5 s gap and a
  backwards timestamp do not advance it; set complete stops it for good; lag wraps round the cycle.

Not done here, on purpose: `FrameResult` does **not** gain `ghost`/`ghostPhase`/`phaseLag`. The
clock needs `setComplete`, which the engine does not know (the prescription lives in the UI), and
the engine is the scoring path this clock must stay out of. 10.9 owns one clock beside the engine
and feeds it `phaseState != IDLE`, `completedRep != null`, and its own set-complete flag.

Commands: `gradlew testDebugUnitTest` - BUILD SUCCESSFUL; 881 tests, 0 failures.

Device gate: **not yet runnable** - nothing draws the clock until 10.9.

### Revised 2026-10-03: the ghost no longer parks

After the first device run the user asked for the opposite of this spec's engagement guard: a
patient who stops does not know how to continue, so the guide must keep demonstrating and prompt
them, not freeze at the start pose. `WAITING` is gone. The clock always cycles; a whole
demonstrated rep with the patient never leaving IDLE sets `GhostTick.patientIdle`, which clears the
moment they move, with no jump in the ghost. The spec's "parks the ghost ... resumes when they move"
test is replaced by: still patient still shown the movement and flagged idle; flag clears on
movement without a jump; a moving patient is never flagged. The trap below about "a ghost looping at
an empty room" is answered by the prompt rather than by stopping.

Run 2026-10-03 22:00 (`frontnormalsr`, prescribed 12): 12/12 reps; lag between patient and guide
mostly -2..+23 phase across the set and not growing - the resync holds; DONE at rep 12 and nothing
drawn after. That is the 10.8 gate ("follows the ghost through a full prescribed set without the
two drifting apart"), passed, on the pre-revision clock; the revision only changes behaviour for a
patient who stands still.

## Traps

- **Scoring keeps looking up the patient's own phase, not the ghost's.** Comparing the patient
  against the ghost's phase makes every z-score blow up the moment they fall behind — it abandons
  §6.8.1's tempo invariance and turns hesitation into a form fault. Tempo is already judged once,
  over the finished rep, by §6.17.3's tempo penalty. Surface the lag to the patient; never score it.
- **Resync on rep completion.** Without it the patient and the ghost drift apart over a set of ten
  and the ghost stops being followable exactly when fatigue makes it most useful.
- **Stop the clock when the set is complete.** A ghost still working through reps is an instruction
  to keep going past the prescription.
- Hold-mode exercises have no phase axis and are out of scope; `HoldMode` keeps its own path.
- The engagement guard is not a nicety. A ghost looping at an empty room is how an overlay becomes
  something the patient learns to ignore.
