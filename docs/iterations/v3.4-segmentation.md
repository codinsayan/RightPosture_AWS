## Result

- Added `core/reference/Segmentation.kt`: `segmentReps` plus `Extremum`, `RepSegment` and
  `SegmentationResult`.
- Added `MIN_SURVIVING_REPS = 4` to `Params.kt`. Every other threshold this iteration needs was
  already in §7.
- §6.7.3 implemented as written: candidates ranked by prominence descending, greedily accepted
  subject to the `0.6 × T` spacing rule, with a `0.25 × swing` prominence floor. The plan calls the
  spacing rule the single highest-value rule in the section, and a test earns that claim by feeding
  a signal with three local maxima per rep and asserting exactly eight reps come out.
- Valleys are found by negating the signal and reusing the peak path. Prominence is a vertical
  distance, so it survives the negation unchanged, and one implementation cannot drift from the
  other.
- §6.7.4 alternation repair inserts the extreme sample between two same-kind extrema, and drops the
  offending pair when there is nothing between them. Keeping the stronger of the two would silently
  bias the rep count.
- §6.7.6 steps 1, 2 and 4 applied: unconditional first/last removal, duration outliers against the
  **median**, and the four-survivor bar. `usable` is emptied when the recording is rejected so a
  caller cannot use partial results by accident, and `rejection` carries a message naming the
  numbers.

### Deferred

§6.7.6 step 3 (shape outliers by RMS deviation from the provisional mean trajectory) is **not**
implemented here. It requires each rep resampled onto a common phase grid (3.5) **and** the median trajectory
and MAD machinery it thresholds against, which arrive in 3.6. It is implemented there.
Building a second, different notion of rep shape now would guarantee the two disagree later.

Corrected from an earlier note in this file that said 3.5: resampling alone is not enough, and
writing a one-off median and MAD in 3.5 would duplicate what 3.6 builds properly.

### Finding: a rep boundary on the first or last sample is undetectable

Not a defect, but it caught out this iteration's own tests and is worth stating. A local extremum
needs a neighbour on both sides, so an extremum landing exactly on sample 0 or the last sample can
never be found — a recording that begins and ends precisely at a rep boundary silently yields one
rep fewer than it contains.

This is benign in practice, and §6.7.6 step 1 already assumes it: real recordings start with the
physio settling into position and end with them reaching for the stop button. The test signals were
corrected to include that lead-in and lead-out rather than the detector being changed to special-case
endpoints, because a synthetic signal that starts exactly on a mathematical extremum is the
unrealistic thing here.
