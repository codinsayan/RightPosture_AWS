# Version 11.2 — Whole-rep conformance gate

Status: in-progress — implemented and unit tested; device gate not yet run.  
Source: §6.12.4, §6.17.2; follows 11.1

Scope: refuse a rep whose conformant share **over the whole rep** is too low, as a backstop beneath
11.1's per-bucket rule. `nonConformantFrames` is already tracked and never used as a gate.

## Why this is not redundant with 11.1

Checkpoints are only required as far as the rep actually reached — `requiredCount` scales with
`phiMax`, deliberately, because a rep that stops short is short, which is `RANGE`'s business rather
than a skipped step's.

So a rep peaking at φ = 0.95 requires three of four buckets, and the top bucket is not required. A
patient who travels correctly and then spends the entire end-range hold out of position clears every
checkpoint that was asked of them. §6.17.2 puts the critical window at end range — that is where the
therapeutic work lives — so this is close to the worst rep the per-bucket rule alone would count.

Tests: the end-range-held-wrongly case counts as `SKIPPED` while reporting every required checkpoint
visited; a faithful rep reports 1.0; a rep nothing could be scored in reports 1.0 and falls through
to `UNSEEN` rather than being accused of a fault nobody saw.

Device gate: perform a rep with good travel and a deliberately wrong end-range hold. It must be
refused, and the reason shown to the patient must name the joint rather than say "you jumped to the
end".

## Result

- `core/live/PhaseTracker.kt` — new `repJudgedFrames`, counted beside `repNonConformantFrames` on
  exactly the frames 11.1 judges (valid, not past `phi = 1`). `completeRep` computes
  `conformantFraction` from it and refuses below the bar as `SKIPPED`, after the checkpoint test
  and before the rate test. 1.0 when nothing was judged.
- `CompletedRep` gains `conformantFraction: Double = 1.0`, **last and defaulted**. Every existing
  construction uses named arguments, so nothing needed updating — including `CueDispatcherTest`,
  which is Track B's file and was deliberately not touched. It is still a shape change to a seam
  type, so Track B should hear about it; nothing in front of the seam reads it yet.
- `core/Params.kt` — `TRAJECTORY_MIN_REP_CONFORMANT_FRACTION` 0.5, under the 0.6 bucket bar.
- `TrajectoryConformanceTest` — 5 new cases (end-range hold wrong → `SKIPPED` with every required
  checkpoint visited; same hold right → counts at 1.0; one lapse in five → counts; dropouts neither
  conformant nor not → `UNSEEN` at 1.0; the bar sits under the bucket bar), plus 1.0 assertions on
  the faithful and overshoot cases. The overshoot one pins the denominator: judged frames, not
  valid frames.

The joint is still named on the refusal: `ExerciseEngine` names `worstJoint` on any `SKIPPED` rep
with `nonConformantFrames > 0`, which this rep always has. No engine change was needed.

Commands:

- `gradlew testDebugUnitTest` — BUILD SUCCESSFUL; 780 tests, 0 failures (774 before, 11.3's
  one new test included).

Device gate: **not yet run.**

## Traps

- **Looser than the per-bucket bar, not tighter.** This is the backstop. Set it above the bucket
  fraction and it starts rejecting reps that were right everywhere the checkpoints looked.
- **Order it after the checkpoint test and before the rate test** in `completeRep`. Range first
  stays first — a rep that never reached the end has a simpler thing wrong with it, and telling a
  patient they skipped the middle of a movement they never finished is the less useful of two true
  statements.
- Same overshoot abstention as 11.1. If 11.1 is implemented first this comes for free; if these are
  done together, do not forget it.
- `CompletedRep` gains a field, which means every test fixture constructing one needs updating —
  `CueDispatcherTest` has one.
