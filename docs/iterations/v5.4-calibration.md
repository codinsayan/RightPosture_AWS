## Notes

- **Each check is tested alone.** A check that only ever passes alongside the other six proves
  nothing about which one is doing the work, so every one of the seven has a test that fails it in
  isolation.
- **An unread sensor fails check 5 rather than passing it.** §6.6's abstention rule cuts both ways:
  not knowing is never the same as agreeing, and a calibration gate that silently passes a check it
  could not measure is not a gate.
- **The two checks with a hold duration reset rather than accumulate.** A patient visible for 1.9 s,
  gone for one frame, then visible for 1.9 s more has not been visible for 2.0 s.
- **The recheck samples sparsely, once every 5 s, and only acts on a confident classification.** A
  frame that cannot be classified at all — a momentary occlusion, the patient mid-stride — is not
  evidence the phone moved. Sampling every frame would pause sets on pose noise.
- **Frames are withheld from the engine while paused.** A rep measured through a camera that has
  moved is not a measurement; banking it would be worse than losing it. Reps already completed are
  kept, so a bump costs the patient the rep in progress and nothing behind it.
