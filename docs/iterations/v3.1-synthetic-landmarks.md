# Version 3.1 — Synthetic landmark generator

Status: complete
Source: §19

Scope: deterministic cyclic landmarks with known rep count, ROM, noise, and dropout.

Tests: seed reproducibility and requested rep/ROM measurements.

Gate: authoring tests run without camera or person.

## Result

- Generates a complete timestamped recording of deterministic right-knee repetitions.
- Configures rep count, even samples per rep, minimum/maximum knee angle, sample rate, Gaussian
  landmark noise, whole-frame dropout probability, and random seed.
- Includes both cycle endpoints, so `N` repetitions contain `N * samplesPerRep + 1` samples.
- Represents dropout with zero visibility/presence while retaining coordinates.
- Produces world landmarks plus a simple normalized image projection without camera dependencies.

## Gate result

- Seed reproducibility: passed with noise and dropout enabled.
- Requested 5 repetitions and 90° ROM recovered from generated knee landmarks.
- Noise and forced dropout injection: passed.
- Full JVM suite: 97 tests, 0 failures.
