# Version 10.7 — Bone-length retargeting

Status: in-progress — implemented and unit tested; device gate not yet run.  
Source: new work; overlaps Track A's C5

Scope: after 10.5 has placed the ghost, walk the bone tree outward from the hips keeping the
reference's **direction** per bone and substituting the patient's **own** bone length.

A uniform torso scale puts the ghost's wrist wherever the physio's arm reached. A longer-limbed
patient can hold a perfect joint angle and still never touch it — chasing a target that cannot be
met, which for a leading ghost means actively teaching the wrong thing rather than merely showing
it. Retargeting preserves the joint angles, which are the thing being taught and the thing being
scored, and makes the target reachable.

This is also a partial answer to Track A's C5 (no per-patient reference offset): a patient built
differently from the physio no longer sits at a systematic offset in the drawn skeleton, even though
they still do in the angle reference.

Tests: a patient with shins half again as long gets a ghost with their shins, not the physio's;
joint angles are unchanged by the retarget; a bone whose patient-side length cannot be measured
keeps its fitted position.

Device gate: two people of visibly different proportions follow the same reference. Both should be
able to reach the ghost.

## Result

- `core/live/GhostRetarget.kt` — `Bone`, `RETARGETED_BONES` (ten limb bones, parents first, no
  torso), `boneLengths(frame)`, `medianBoneLengths(frames)`, and
  `PlacedGhost.retargeted(pose, patientLengths)`.
- `core/Params.kt` — `GHOST_RETARGET_MAX_RATIO` 1.6: a ratio past it is a landmark that slid, not
  a differently built person, and the bone keeps the physio's length. Uncalibrated.
- `camera/GhostCheckScreen.kt` (Track B) — every placed ghost is retargeted to the median limb
  lengths of the recording on screen, so "Ghost from" someone else's saved exercise shows it in the
  current person's proportions.

**Two decisions the spec did not make.**

1. **3D lengths, not image lengths.** An image length shrinks whenever the limb points at the
   camera, so measuring there would shrink the ghost's forearm on every frame the patient reached
   towards the lens — the reverted attempt did exactly that. The ratio is taken of world-space
   lengths, each body in its own torso lengths; projection is linear, so stretching the *drawn*
   bone by that ratio is exact and foreshortening cannot leak in.
2. **Lengths are measured separately and smoothed by the caller.** `retargeted` takes a length map;
   the check screen passes a median over the whole recording. Per-frame lengths would make the
   ghost's limbs pulse with MediaPipe's jitter. The live overlay (10.9) needs a running median.

**One finding: the ghost has no heels.** `GHOST_LANDMARKS` carries what features are computed from
and what the skeleton draws; ankle dorsiflexion uses the foot index, so landmarks 29/30 are absent —
visible in the 2026-09-30 device log's landmark list too. The foot bone is ankle → foot index, and a
test pins that every bone end is carried. Written first with heels, seven tests failed on it.

Unmeasured bones keep their fitted length **relative to their parent**, not their fitted absolute
position — otherwise a retargeted shin would leave an unmeasured foot behind, detached.

`GhostRetargetTest` — 12 cases: 1.5× shins land the ghost's ankles and feet on the patient's; the
same fixture without retargeting misses (so the first cannot pass vacuously); joint angles
unchanged; torso and nose untouched; an unseen ankle keeps the physio's shin; an unseen foot is
carried by its moved ankle; a 3× shin is distrusted; a forearm pointing at the camera is not read as
short; lengths are size-invariant; the median ignores a bad frame; every bone end is carried;
parents precede children.

Commands: `gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug` — BUILD SUCCESSFUL; 865 tests, 0 failures.

Device gate: **not yet run.** Person A records and saves an exercise. Person B, of visibly
different proportions, records the same exercise, opens "Check the ghost", and picks A's exercise
under "Ghost from". The ghost's joints should sit on B's joints — not merely B's torso.

## Traps

- **Parents must be placed before children.** Order the bone list so one forward pass suffices — an
  elbow can only be positioned once the shoulder it hangs from is.
- **Exclude the torso cross-members.** Hip width and shoulder width are already fixed by the fit's
  anchors; re-deriving them fights the fit.
- **Keep the fitted position when a length cannot be measured**, rather than dropping the bone. The
  scale is already approximately right, and a missing forearm is more confusing to a patient than a
  slightly long one.
- This is where the "componentwise median is not strictly a pose" problem from 10.2 gets fixed. Do
  not try to fix it at storage time instead — the bone lengths there are the physio's, and the ones
  that matter are the patient's.
