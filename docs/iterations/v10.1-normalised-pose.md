# Version 10.1 — Normalised pose

Status: complete — tests only, as §1.1 allows for a pure-core iteration.  
Source: §6.2, §6.4; new work for the leading ghost

Scope: a body-frame pose type and the two operations on it. `NormalisedPose`,
`normalisedPose(frame, bodyFrame)`, `NormalisedPose.mirrored()`, the ghost landmark subset and its
slot maps. Pure core, no Android, no storage, nothing drawn.

Origin at the hip midpoint, axes from §6.4's body frame, scaled so the torso is one unit:

```
p_body = BodyFrame.toBody(world[i] − midHip) / |midShoulder − midHip|
```

All three normalisations are load-bearing. Without the origin the ghost is pinned to wherever the
physio stood; without the rotation it faces whichever way their camera did; without the torso scale
it is drawn at their body size and their distance rather than the patient's.

Tests: a pose is unchanged by where the body stands, which way it faces and how big it is;
mirroring swaps left and right labels *and* negates x; mirroring twice is identity; a degenerate
torso returns null rather than a pose.

Device gate: none. No Android dependency, so §1.1's rule applies — this is gated by tests alone.

## Result

- `core/pose/NormalisedPose.kt` — `NormalisedPose` (with `get(landmarkId)`), `normalisedPose`,
  `mirrored()`, `GHOST_LANDMARKS`, `GHOST_LANDMARK_SLOT`, `GHOST_MIRROR_SLOT`.
- `GHOST_LANDMARKS` is **derived**: nose + the twelve drawn joints + `contributingLandmarks` of
  every feature on both sides. The drawn joints are restated in core, not imported from `pose/`
  (core must not depend on the Android package); a test pins the two lists together.
- `normalisedPose` also returns null on a non-finite landmark, not only on a short torso.
- Mirror = relabel + negate body x, y and z untouched. Derived from reflecting the world and
  relabelling (which flips the body frame's x axis with it), and tested against exactly that.
- `NormalisedPoseTest` — 15 cases: translation, rotation (70° about vertical plus 15° lean) and
  scale invariance; unit torso at the origin; image landmarks ignored; mirror agrees with a
  reflected body; mirror moves the raised arm across; mirror is not a bare negation; mirror twice
  is identity; every carried landmark's opposite is carried; collapsed torso and NaN landmark →
  null; drawn bones, feature landmarks carried; face past the nose excluded.
- Fixture has the right arm raised. A symmetric body passes every mirror test with the relabel
  missing.

Commands: `gradlew testDebugUnitTest` — BUILD SUCCESSFUL; 807 tests, 0 failures.

## Traps

- **`worldLandmarks` only, never `imageLandmarks`.** The image ones are perspective-divided and
  their z is not in the same unit as their x and y, so a body frame built from them is a basis over
  three unrelated quantities — and §6.4's degeneracy guards would not notice.
- **A mirror is not a negation.** Negating x without relabelling leaves a left knee sitting where
  the right one belongs and every bone drawn to the wrong joint. Same trap §6.2 names for the live
  feed; the relabel and the negation are one operation.
- **Not all 33 landmarks.** The eleven face points past the nose and the finger points are noise at
  this distance and no §6.5 feature depends on them. Store the union of the drawn skeleton and
  `contributingLandmarks`. The nose stays — it is the only landmark above the shoulders and it is
  what tells a sagittal view which way the body faces (10.6).
