## Superseded, for the ghost half

§6.16's ghost here is the plan's original one: chained off the arrow, following the patient's own
phase. The ghost that is actually wanted **leads** — it runs at the physio's recorded tempo and the
patient follows it — which needs a stored skeleton trajectory and a time axis the bundle does not
carry. That work is [10.1 to 10.9](README.md), and the ghost should be built there rather than here.

The arrow half of this iteration is unaffected and still outstanding. Note that 10.5 exists because
of what happened to it: an exactly-determined fit that cannot report its own error needs a sign
check a human looks at, or it ends up built, tested and switched off.
