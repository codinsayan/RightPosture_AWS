### §6.7.6 step 3, deferred from 3.4 and 3.5, now implemented

Shape outliers needed both the resampled phase grid (3.5) and the median trajectory plus MAD that
this iteration builds. Each rep's RMS deviation from the provisional median trajectory of the
dominant feature is compared against `median + 2.5 × MAD`.

### Finding: a zero MAD must not skip outlier rejection

The first implementation bailed out when MAD came to zero, on the reasoning that there was no
dispersion to threshold against. That is backwards, and a test caught it: with five identical reps
and one of a different shape, MAD is exactly zero, and bailing out kept the very rep the step exists
to remove.

With a zero MAD the threshold equals the centre, which is the correct behaviour in both directions —
identical reps all pass, because their deviation of 0 is not greater than 0, while a differently
shaped rep still fails. §6.22's "MAD = 0 applies the σ floor" rule is about dispersion estimates,
not about this filter, and reading it as covering both is what produced the bug.
