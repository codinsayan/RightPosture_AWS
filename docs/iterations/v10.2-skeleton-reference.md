# Version 10.2 — Skeleton reference builder

Status: complete — device gate passed 2026-09-30.  
Source: §6.8, §6.9; new work for the leading ghost

Scope: build the physio's body over one rep, on the §6.8.4 phase grid, from the same reps §6.9 built
the angle reference from. Per bin: the median pose, per-landmark spread, per-landmark minimum
visibility, and whether any contributing frame had a `FRESH` body frame.

Nothing in the bundle stores a position today — the reference is fifteen independent scalar curves,
which is enough to *score* a pose and nothing like enough to *draw* one. No angle reference can be
inverted back into a skeleton without limb lengths it never stored.

Reuse `phaseProfile` and `interpolateOntoBins` from 3.5 (the latter needs widening to `internal`).
Landmark coordinates are just more per-frame scalar channels.

Tests: 101 finite bins per channel; a rep with an unusable body frame anywhere is dropped rather
than interpolated across the hole; fewer than `MIN_SURVIVING_REPS` usable reps returns null rather
than a thin reference.

Device gate: record a real exercise and log the result — bin count, the range of per-bin spread, and
the minimum visibility per landmark. Spread should be small on the joints the physio controlled and
large on the ones they did not. A reference where everything is equally scattered means the builder
is measuring noise.

## Result

- `core/reference/SkeletonReference.kt` — `SkeletonReference` (flat `positions` `[bin][slot][xyz]`,
  `spread` and `visibility` `[bin][slot]`, `fresh` per bin, `repCount`; `poseAt`, `spreadAt`,
  `visibilityAt`, which reject an out-of-grid bin rather than clamp), `buildSkeletonReference`, and
  `diagnostic()` for the device log.
- `core/reference/PhaseResampling.kt` — `interpolateOntoBins` widened to `internal`.
- `core/reference/AuthoringPipeline.kt` — `AuthoringResult.skeleton`, defaulted and last, built
  after the reference. Null does not fail authoring.
- `camera/CameraPreviewScreen.kt` (**Track B's file**, one helper and two calls) — logs
  `diagnostic()` under `RPGhost` once per finished authoring run. Recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.

**One decision the spec did not make:** landmark channels are filtered with §6.3.2's zero-phase
Butterworth at the features' cutoff, per rep, before resampling. Spread is meant to be rep-to-rep
variation, as sigma is; unfiltered it would mostly measure MediaPipe's frame jitter, which is
exactly the gate's failure case ("everything equally scattered").

Deliberately left for later iterations: time→phase profile and rep duration (10.3), mirrored-source
bit (10.6), bundle entry (10.4).

`SkeletonReferenceTest` — 12 cases. The one that matters: knee and elbow angles measured off the
skeleton lie on a straight line in `p` within 3°, which a time-indexed resample misses by ~19°.
Also: grid shape and finiteness; return half closes on the start pose; a rep with one invalid body
frame is dropped; below `MIN_SURVIVING_REPS` → null; all-`REUSED` still builds with `fresh` false;
one atypical rep does not move the median; spread rises only on the inconsistent landmark; lowest
visibility, not mean; mismatched inputs → null; the diagnostic names every landmark.

Commands: `gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug` — BUILD SUCCESSFUL; 807 tests, 0 failures.

Device gate: **passed, 2026-09-30** (`docs/device-run-log.md`, run 00:55). A frontal, front-camera
arm exercise: `bins=101 reps=4 fresh=101/101`. Median spread in torso lengths runs from 0.003 at the
hips (at the origin by construction) and 0.010 at the shoulders, through 0.025–0.046 on the still
legs and feet (landmark noise), to **0.082 median / 0.169 max on the moving left wrist** (15) and
0.033 on its elbow. A gradient tracking the movement, not uniform scatter. Lowest visibility 0.86.

Caveat worth keeping: in one recording "controlled tightly" cannot be told apart from "did not
move", so this confirms the builder measures the physio and not noise — not that spread separates a
controlled joint from a sloppy one. That would need a deliberately sloppy recording.

## Traps

- **Resample through the same interpolation the features use.** A ghost resampled by any other rule
  drifts out of step with the z-scores it is meant to be a picture of, and the disagreement is
  invisible — both halves look individually right. §6.8.1 calls this the subtlest correctness issue
  in the system and it applies here unchanged.
- **Median, not mean**, for the reason §6.9.1 gives: with five to seven reps one atypical rep still
  moves a mean noticeably.
- **Minimum visibility across reps, not mean.** One rep where the joint was hidden is a reason to
  doubt the ghost there, and a mean averages that doubt away.
- **Spread as a distance, not per-axis.** A landmark two centimetres out is two centimetres out
  whichever way it went, and three separate axis deviations have to be recombined at every read to
  mean anything.
- **A componentwise median across reps is not strictly a pose** — bone lengths drift slightly
  between bins. Do not try to fix that here; it is 10.7's job, at draw time.
- **`FRESH` versus `REUSED` is provenance, not a rejection.** A sagittal recording is mostly
  `REUSED` by design (§6.4). Record it; do not filter on it.
